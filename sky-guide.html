BEGINNER OBSERVER

HOW TO START.

You don't need expensive equipment to begin exploring the night sky.

FIND DARK SKIES

01

Move away from strong city lights when possible. Even a small reduction in light pollution can reveal more stars.

START WITH THE MOON

02

The Moon is bright, easy to find and full of visible features that make it perfect for beginners.

LEARN THE CONSTELLATIONS

03

Learn a few recognizable patterns first. They become useful landmarks for finding other objects.

GIVE YOUR EYES TIME

04

Spend several minutes outside before judging how many stars you can see. Your eyes gradually adapt to darkness.
