GET TO KNOW US

A team of students from different fields, brought together by the same question: what’s out there? We come from different academic backgrounds, but share a curiosity about everything from planets and stars to galaxies, black holes, and the physics that holds it all together. We learn from one another, explore ideas together, and make room for anyone who wants to look a little closer at the universe.
